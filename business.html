Gateway

Business

Gateway is an open-source project by XPDevs.

Gateway is not a commercial product — it is a free, open-source project built to demonstrate a privacy-first alternative to traditional search engines. There are no business accounts, no paid tiers, and no enterprise licensing.

For developers

Gateway is fully open source under the MIT license. You are free to fork, modify, self-host, or integrate it into your own projects. The source code is available on GitHub.
