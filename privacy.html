Gateway

Privacy

Gateway is designed to be private by default. No accounts, no tracking, no data collection.

What Gateway collects

Nothing. Gateway runs entirely in your browser with a local search index. There is no backend server, no database, and no analytics. Your search queries never leave your device.

Local storage

Gateway uses your browser's localStorage and IndexedDB to remember your preferences (language, dark mode) and to store the search index for offline use. This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time from your browser settings.

No third-party services

Gateway performs all searches against a bundled local index. No network requests are made when you search — your queries stay on your device and are never sent to any external service.

No cookies

Gateway does not set any cookies.
