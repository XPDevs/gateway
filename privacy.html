Gateway

Privacy

Gateway is designed to be private by default. No accounts, no tracking, no data collection.

What Gateway collects

Nothing. Gateway runs entirely in your browser. There is no backend server, no database, and no analytics. Your search queries are never sent to Gateway — they go directly to the sources you search (Wikipedia, DuckDuckGo, Wikimedia Commons) and are subject to their privacy policies.

Local storage

Gateway uses your browser's localStorage to remember your preferences (language, dark mode, cached search results). This data never leaves your device and can be cleared at any time from your browser settings.

Third-party sources

When you search, your query is sent to the following third-party services:

  • Wikipedia — via the MediaWiki API
  • DuckDuckGo — via the HTML search endpoint
  • Wikimedia Commons — via the MediaWiki API

Each service operates under its own privacy policy. Gateway does not control what these services log.

No cookies

Gateway does not set any cookies of its own. Third-party sources may set cookies according to their own policies.
